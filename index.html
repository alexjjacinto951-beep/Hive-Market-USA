What We Buy & Trade

We purchase upfront for instant cash, Venmo/Zelle, or 1.5x store trade credit for curated vintage and streetwear garments.

Classification Accepted Styles & Era Evaluation Payout
Graphic Tees Single-stitch, band tees, 90s anime, Harley Davidson, racing jerseys, varsity outerwear. Highest Payout
Denim & Workwear Vintage Levi's 501/550, Carhartt double-knee pants and jackets, Dickies, military surplus. High Demand
Footwear Doc Martens, leather loafers, clean Nike Dunks, Jordan 1s, retro New Balance, work boots. High Demand
Accessories Y2K studded bags, genuine leather belts, vintage snapbacks, trucker hats, silver jewelry. Always Buying
Declined Items Ultra fast-fashion (Shein, Temu, Forever 21), damaged garments, unwashed apparel. No Buyout
