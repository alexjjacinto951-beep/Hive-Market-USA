Sell & Trade Guide

Bring in your curated archive items, vintage t-shirts, rugged workwear, and footwear for instant cash or elevated store credit.

1. Organize Your Clothes
We have a 40 item limit per appointment, so select your best pieces!

Clothing, footwear, and accessories that are:

  • Clean: Freshly laundered & odor free.
  • In Great Condition: No unintended holes, heavy stains, or hardware breakage.
  • In Season & Timely: 90s vintage tees, Carhartt work jackets, denim, and knitwear.
  • Trending Streetwear & Archive: Single-stitch band/anime/skate tees, Y2K items, and Doc Martens.
  • Designer & Select Workwear: Genuine leather goods, military surplus, and curated archive jackets.

We currently do not purchase:

  • Ultra fast-fashion (Shein, Temu, Forever 21, Fashion Nova, basic Zara).
  • Children’s clothes, sleepwear, or undergarments.
  • Formal suits, gowns, and corporate business attire.
  • Swimwear, household goods, or unwashed garments.
2. Bring Your ID or Passport

Valid government-issued identification is required for all appointments, trade drop-offs, and cash payouts. You must be 18 years or older to sell.

3. Claim Your Cash or Credit

Each selected item is priced individually by our buyers. Receive 30% cash/Venmo/Zelle of our selling price on the spot, or take 50% in store trade credit to spend anytime.

Book Your Appointment

Read the guidelines above before reserving your slot. Walk-ins are always welcomed during open hours, but booking guarantees an expedited appraisal.

WHAT TO KNOW BEFORE YOU BOOK: Please ensure your maximum 40 items are freshly washed, placed neatly in bags or bins, and that you have your valid ID ready upon arrival at 688 Beaumont Ave.

Who We Are

The Hive Market story begins in Beaumont, California, rooted in a shared passion for archive street culture, vintage preservation, and revitalizing in-person subcultural discovery. Founded by Marissa and Jordan, the space was envisioned as more than a standard vintage retail shop—it was created to be a style haven and creative sanctuary celebrating authenticity and self-expression. Offering curated single-stitch t-shirts, Carhartt workwear, rugged denim, and timeless leather boots, Hive Market inspires collectors and visitors to build one-of-a-kind looks every time they walk through the doors.

From hand-picking every single garment on the racks to hosting outdoor sidewalk fleas and intimate in-store concert sound sessions, Marissa & Jordan continue to shape the cultural heartbeat of 688 Beaumont Ave. Whether you are crate-digging through 90s band tees, finding a rare jacket, listening to analog vinyl sets, or meeting independent regional designers at our weekend pop-ups, Hive Market remains a physical home for community and sound.

Every curation, concert night, and buyout is driven by circular fashion—giving iconic streetwear garments a second life while cultivating an inclusive gathering place where musicians, artists, and vintage enthusiasts can connect under one roof.
