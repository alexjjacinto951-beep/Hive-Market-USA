Sell & Trade With Us

Book a dedicated buyer evaluation slot or walk in any day during store hours.

The Selling Process

  • 1 Bring up to 2 clean bags/bins of vintage clothing, shoes, or accessories.
  • 2 Appraisal in 15–20 minutes while you browse the $10 racks or chill in our audio lounge.
  • 3 Get paid on the spot: Cash, Venmo/Zelle, or take 1.5x in Store Trade Credit.
