About Hive Market

A place for clothes, creativity, and community.

What started as selling clothes out of a garage became something much bigger than we ever imagined. In the early days, our goal was simple: collect clothes we loved, share them with people who appreciated them, and create a community around them. Building that community felt almost impossible during COVID, but the idea never went away.

In January 2026, we stumbled across a space in Beaumont, California that felt like the perfect place to bring that vision to life. What followed was five months of remodeling, building, designing, and figuring things out along the way. On June 11, 2026, Hive Market officially opened its doors at 688 Beaumont Ave.

What We Do

We operate at the intersection of vintage clothing, rugged workwear, skate culture, and independent creators from our region and beyond.

Instead of treating clothing as something disposable, we believe in giving things a second life. Everything we bring into Hive is carefully selected because it has a story, a character, or a place within the culture we love. Every garment on our racks is hand-picked and curated by us. Nothing is here simply to fill a rack.

More Than a Store

As long-time collectors and curators, Marissa and Jordan started Hive with the mission to build a physical haven where independent designers, musicians, vintage lovers, and creatives could connect under one roof, and create something meaningful in Beaumont.

That mission continues through everything we do. From community flea markets and pop-ups to in-store concerts, DJ events, creative collaborations, and sound sessions, we’re constantly looking for new ways to bring people together.

  • We believe a great space can do more than sell things.
  • It can inspire you.
  • It can introduce you to people.
  • It can give you a place to belong.
  • It can make you want to create something of your own.

Our Vision

Our goal is to create a community that welcomes everyone and an environment where creativity can blossom. Whether you’re digging through the racks, discovering a new artist, listening to music, selling your own work, meeting friends, or simply hanging out, we want Hive to feel like a place you can make your own. We’re building more than a vintage shop, we’re building a home for culture, creativity, and community in Beaumont.
